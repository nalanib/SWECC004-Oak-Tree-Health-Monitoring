# Visual crown assessment

Crown condition scored in the field on the ICP Forests scheme by two independent
observers, allowing an inter-observer agreement check. This is the most directly
visual indicator of tree vitality in the study.

## Files

- `crown_raw.csv` — individual observer scores per tree
- `crown_per_tree.csv` — per-tree means (defoliation class and %, transparency, dieback, epicormic shoots, number of observations)
- `SWECC004_visual_crown_description.docx` — written methods and results description

## Method notes

Each tree was scored for crown defoliation, transparency, and dieback (all as
percentages in 5 % steps) and for epicormic shoot development (0–2 scale).
Scoring covered the seven study trees plus two additional healthy-reference
candidates (HT08 and HT09). Reported values are the mean of the two observers;
the absolute observer difference is retained as a precision check.

## Key result

Group mean defoliation: untreated 59.2 %, bokashi 37.5 %, healthy-reference
candidates 15.8 %. The 21.7-percentage-point separation between bokashi and
untreated trees is the largest treatment-level effect anywhere in the study.
Mean absolute inter-observer differences were small (defoliation 3.9,
transparency 5.6, dieback 3.3 percentage points).
